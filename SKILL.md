## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a poetic emblem of a feeling, not a picture of an object. One large biomorphic field holds one tiny, highly recognizable focus. Use negative space, arcs and a few geometric cuts. Material is matte pigment, paper grain and a light screen-print feeling. Keep the density very low, like a scent-brand or art-institution mark. Reject a literal object, a busy scene, smooth vector and piled symbols.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
